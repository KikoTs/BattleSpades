## Later on 2026-09-21: locomotion rework

The first rendered playtest of that build was stopped after 40 s: bots "spin
around", were slow and did not move like players. Two passes on travel
followed; see `BOT_LOCOMOTION_2026-09-21.md` for causes, numbers and tests.
In short: routes are steered at a far point instead of cell by cell, small
ledges are run off, movement keys reproduce the requested heading wherever the
eyes look, the eyes follow one rule (last-seen enemy, then the route ahead),
and corridor guidance is planned a stretch at a time. Quiet travel on
AncientEgypt went from 4.4 to 7.5 blocks/s and there-and-back head wags from 25
to 11 per bot-minute. Measure with `scripts/bot_motion_review.py`; the trace's
`navigation.step_note` says how each route step is being taken.
All five excavated-pocket fixtures pass; the long-red Mayan one was a planner
drop into a tunnel mouth plus a straight walk under a low ceiling.
